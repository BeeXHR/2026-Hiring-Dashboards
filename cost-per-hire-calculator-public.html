    How the numbers are worked out

    Gross cost = monthly salary × period + AWS + employer CPF + agency fee.

    Net cost = gross cost − funding received. Funding covers monthly salary only, not AWS, CPF or fees.

    Employer CPF is charged on salary up to the monthly ordinary-wage ceiling, and on AWS up to the annual ceiling minus the year’s CPF-able salary.

    The agency fee is a one-off charge in month 1. Figures are rounded to the nearest dollar for display.
